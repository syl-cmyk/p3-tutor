# The pinch grip and the claw grip

The learner can hold a knife with a pinch grip on the blade and hold the food with a claw grip, with the fingertips curled back.
The learner sees that the knuckles guide the side of the blade, which keeps the fingertips out of its way.

**Evidence:** Showed the grip on a carrot and explained why the fingertips stay curled back.

**Implications:** Which knife and cut to use for each job, and how to keep a knife sharp and store it, have not been taught.
