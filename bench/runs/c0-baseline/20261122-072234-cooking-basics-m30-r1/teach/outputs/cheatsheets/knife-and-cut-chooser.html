Knife and cut chooser

From Lesson 1. Pick the cut from the dish, then the knife from the food.

Knives

KnifeUse it for
Chef's (8–10 in)Big, firm food: onions, carrots, potatoes, squash.
Paring (2–4 in)Small, close work: peeling, trimming, coring, hulling.
SerratedTough or slippery outside, soft inside: bread, tomatoes.

Cuts

CutShapeTypical size
SliceFlat pieceThin or thick, as the dish needs
JulienneThin matchsticks1/8 in square, 2–2½ in long
BatonnetSticks1/4 in square, about 2–3 in long
Small diceCubes1/4 in
Medium diceCubes1/2 in
Large diceCubes3/4 in

Three questions

  1. What shape does the dish want? (the cut)
  2. How big and firm is the food? (chef's or paring)
  3. Is the outside tougher than the inside? (serrated)

Sizes vary a little between books; treat them as targets. Sources: Rouxbe, Wikipedia.
