# The purpose of the claw grip is not yet secure

On quiz 0001 the learner knew the knuckles guide the blade, but chose a flat, fingers-out hand for the food and said the fingertips curl back "so the knife does not slip". This differs from the first-session record, so the purpose of the claw (fingertips kept out of the blade's path) needs revisiting before building on it.

**Evidence:** quiz 0001, submission s1, items q1 (wrong), q2 (correct), q3 (wrong).

**Implications:** Before teaching knife choice and cuts, revisit the claw grip with a short practice. Do not assume the safe hold is automatic.
