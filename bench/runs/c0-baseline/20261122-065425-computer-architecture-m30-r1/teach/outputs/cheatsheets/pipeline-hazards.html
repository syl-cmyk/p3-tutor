Pipeline hazards

From lesson 1. Five stages: IF, ID, EX, MEM, WB.

HazardWaiting forCost in the lesson's pipeline
Structuralhardware another instruction is using1 cycle per conflict (Figure 1)

Formulas

  • Total cycles = stages + instructions − 1 + stall cycles
  • CPI = 1 + (fraction of instructions that stall × stall cycles each)

Diagnose

Ask: what is this instruction waiting for? A resource, a value, or the next address.
