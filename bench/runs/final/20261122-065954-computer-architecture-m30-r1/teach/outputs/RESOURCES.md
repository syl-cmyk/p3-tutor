## Knowledge

- [Slides: "Pipeline Hazards", University of Utah CS 3810](https://users.cs.utah.edu/~aburtsev/3810/lectures/lecture12-hazards/lecture12-hazards.pdf)
  Course lecture on the 5-stage pipeline and the three hazard kinds. Use for: definitions of structural, data and control hazards, unified memory as a structural hazard, half-cycle register access.
- [Article: "Hazard (computer architecture)", Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazard_(computer_architecture))
  Short reference on hazards, bubbles and read-after-write. Use for: quick definitions; it gives no cycle counts.
