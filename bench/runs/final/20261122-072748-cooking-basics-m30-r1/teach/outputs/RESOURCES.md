# Knife Skills Resources

## Knowledge

- [Article: "Different Knives and the Best Uses for Each One" (Escoffier)](https://escoffier.edu/blog/culinary-arts/different-knives-and-the-best-uses-for-each)
  Culinary-school guide to chef's, paring and serrated knives: blade sizes, best uses, what not to use each for. Use for: choosing a knife for a job.
- [Article: "Types of Knife Cuts" (Rouxbe)](https://rouxbe.com/tips-techniques/495-types-of-knife-cuts)
  Sizes for dice, batonnet, julienne and fine julienne, and why uniform pieces cook evenly. Use for: choosing and sizing a cut.
- [Article: "Knife Skills: 10 Knife Cuts Every Professional Cook Should Know" (Escoffier)](https://www.escoffier.edu/?p=23845)
  Escoffier's own cut sizes, including julienne at 2 1/2 in long. Use for: checking a cut's size against a second school.
- [Reference: "List of culinary knife cuts" (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_culinary_knife_cuts)
  Size ranges for many cuts, with the allumette and batonnet names. Use for: a quick look-up of a cut's name and rough size.
