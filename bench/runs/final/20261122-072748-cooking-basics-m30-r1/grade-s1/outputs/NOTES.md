# Notes

## What the learner knows
- How to cut vegetables with a kitchen knife, from years of home cooking.
- Which knife in the drawer is big and which is small.
- The pinch grip and the claw grip, taught in the last session.
- The knuckles guide the side of the blade (quiz 0001, q2).

## What the learner does not know yet
- Which knife suits which job, and what slice, dice and julienne mean.
- How to keep a knife sharp, and the difference between honing and sharpening.
- Why the claw grip works: picked a flat, stretched-out hand for the food and said the fingertips curl "so the knife does not slip", not to keep them out of the blade's path (quiz 0001, q1 and q3).
- Tends to think a dull knife is safer, and that honing a knife sharpens it.
