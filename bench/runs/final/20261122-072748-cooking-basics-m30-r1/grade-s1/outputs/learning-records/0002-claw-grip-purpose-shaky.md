# Claw grip: knows the knuckle guide, not the purpose

On quiz 0001 the learner knew the knuckles guide the side of the blade, but chose a flat, stretched-out hand for holding the food and said the fingertips curl "so the knife does not slip". They do not yet see that the claw keeps the fingertips out of the blade's path.
This softens record 0001: the grip was shown, but the reason behind it is not secure, so revisit it before moving on to cuts.
